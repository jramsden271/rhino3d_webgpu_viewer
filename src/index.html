    Controls

    Orbit
    Right-drag (around the point under the cursor) · Alt + left-drag · one finger
    Pan
    Left- or middle-drag · Shift + right-drag · two fingers
    Zoom
    Mouse wheel (towards the cursor) · pinch on touch
    Walk
    Walk on the toolbar (perspective only) drops you onto the surface below the camera · WASD or arrow keys to walk · drag to look · Shift to run · wheel for speed · Esc to stop · the Walk panel sets the speed, turns collisions off and shows whether you are in sunlight
    Inspect
    Click an object to select it and see its details · Shift-click to add or remove · H hide selected · Esc to clear
    File
    Click the file name (top left) for properties, saving a copy and reverting changes
    Colours
    Click a layer's square in the Layers panel: display colour in Shaded view, material and magic material in Rendered view · Magic ✨ on the toolbar turns magic materials on or off · select an object to change its own colour or material source, or explode a block
    Focus
    Double-click a surface · double-click empty space to zoom extents
    Undo
    Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo (colour, material, block and sun location changes; not views, hiding or layer visibility) · Ctrl+A select all visible objects
    Keys
    F extents · 1 Top · 2 Front · 3 Right · 4 Perspective · P parallel/perspective · M shading mode · E edges · C curves · O ambient occlusion · X x-ray · L layers · S sun
